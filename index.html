How to use Browse Skills

How it works

  1. Open a playbook’s SKILL.md (or copy its URL)
  2. Ask your Grok Bot to read it and run it once on a safe example. Do not send, post, or delete.
  3. The Bot can read the library. It does not gain skills until you save one through Grok Bot (Settings → Plugins, or “Save the process we just used as a skill called …”)
